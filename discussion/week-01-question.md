title: "When Should We Trust an AI Statistical Answer?"
author: "Shushi Meng (shushim2)"
---

Suppose an AI gives a confident explanation and working code for a statistical-learning problem, but its conclusion is wrong. Which verification method should we try first—checking the mathematics, testing a small simulated example with a fixed seed, comparing documentation, or constructing a counterexample—and why? Can we design a short workflow that combines at least two of these checks without simply trusting another AI response?
